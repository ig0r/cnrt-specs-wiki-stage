PennDOT Pavement Policy Manual (Publication 242)

penndotpublication-242pavement-policy-manualflexible-pavementrigid-pavementpavement-management
Created 2026-10-26 · Updated 2026-10-26

Root of the concept hierarchy extracted from PennDOT Publication 242, Pavement Policy Manual (Change 10), for flexible (asphalt) and rigid (concrete) pavements. It joins the four topics the 26 pages cover: designing the pavement structure and its layers (ESALs, Design Structural Number, DARWin, drainage, joints, frost design, mixtures), assessing the condition of existing pavements (distress surveys, IRI), preserving structurally sound pavements with preventive treatments, and rehabilitating deficient pavements, mainly with overlays, chosen by life-cycle cost. The scope is what the manual covers, not pavement engineering as a whole.

PennDOT publications covering “PennDOT Pavement Policy Manual (Publication 242)” also include the following subtopics: Pavement Condition Assessment, Pavement Design, Pavement Preservation, and Rehabilitation.
