Ec, Concrete Elastic Modulus

Ec=57,000(fc′)0.5

This formula computes the Concrete Elastic Modulus, a measure of stiffness or rigidity of concrete, from the PCC compressive strength. As the PCC compressive strength grows, with other factors held constant, the Concrete Elastic Modulus rises.
