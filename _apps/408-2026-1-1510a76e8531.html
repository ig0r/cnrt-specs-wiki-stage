AsphaltTonnage, Asphalt Tonnage

AsphaltTonnage=(0.004164)(A)(a)(g)

This formula computes the equivalent asphalt tonnage for coatings or mixtures measured on a square yard basis. It uses surface area in square yards, gallons per square yard, and specific gravity of the asphalt material. As surface area increases, the asphalt tonnage rises. As gallons per square yard increase, the asphalt tonnage rises. As specific gravity increases, the asphalt tonnage rises.
