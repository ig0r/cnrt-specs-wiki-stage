Lp, Lot Payment

Lp=Cp[2Ps+2Pd400]

This formula computes Lot Payment using the contract price per lot, the characteristic percentage of compressive strength, and the characteristic percentage for depth. As the contract price per lot increases, the lot payment rises. Similarly, an increase in either the characteristic percentage of compressive strength or the characteristic percentage for depth causes the lot payment to rise.
