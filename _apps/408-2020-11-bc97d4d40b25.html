X, Compactionpercentage %, %

X=ρwρt×100

The formula computes the compaction percentage using the recorded density and the computed RCC unit weight. As the recorded density increases, the compaction percentage rises. Conversely, as the computed RCC unit weight increases, the compaction percentage falls.
