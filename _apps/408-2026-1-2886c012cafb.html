LotPayment, Lot Payment

LotPayment=CP(2PD+PB+PA)/400

This formula computes the lot payment based on the contract unit price per lot, the payment factor percentage for density, the payment factor percentage for asphalt content, and the payment factor percentage for percent passing the No. 200 sieve. As any of these inputs increase while others are held constant, the resulting lot payment rises.
