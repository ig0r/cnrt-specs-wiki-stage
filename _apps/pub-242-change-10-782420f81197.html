AAM, Asphalt Adjustment Multiplier

AAM=1+Adjustments/Total of Asphalt Payments.

The Asphalt Adjustment Multiplier is computed from asphalt price adjustments and the total of asphalt payments made over the preceding twelve months. As the asphalt price adjustments increase, the multiplier rises. Conversely, as the total of asphalt payments increases, the multiplier falls.
